Why This Matters

Construction problems often begin as small misunderstandings.

An unclear responsibility. A scope item understood differently by two people. A change whose full effect has not been considered. A payment request that needs clarification. A schedule assumption. A difference between the plans, proposal and contract.

The objective is not to create conflict. It is to help you understand the project while there are still choices available.

How I Help

Independent does not mean disconnected.

With your authorization, I can review the information, speak with relevant project parties when clarification is needed, compare what I learn with the project documents and explain the issue to you in straightforward language.
