Why Construction Intelligence Services

Construction Intelligence Services is founded on practical construction management experience developed throughout more than three decades in the construction industry.

Our experience includes schools, colleges, parks, libraries, civic facilities, and other publicly funded projects, together with residential construction supporting homeowners, contractors, and property improvements.

Working with owners, architects, engineers, contractors, inspectors, and public agencies has provided a practical understanding of how successful projects are planned, communicated, documented, and completed.

That experience is applied with one objective: provide independent construction guidance that helps owners understand their projects, support informed decisions, and contribute to an organized and successful construction process.
