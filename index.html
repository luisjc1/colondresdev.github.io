Illustrative example

An owner receives a substantial change order.

The decision is not simply whether the price feels reasonable. The first question is what the project documents actually support.

I would compare the proposed change with the original scope, proposal, contract documents, prior changes and available supporting information before the owner approves additional money.

What actually changed?
Was any of this already included?
What work is genuinely new?
What supports the cost and schedule impact?

The objective is not to create conflict. It is to help the owner understand the decision before additional money is committed.
