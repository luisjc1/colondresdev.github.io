FREQUENTLY ASKED

Straight answers before we begin.

Are you a contractor?+

No. This service does not bid, contract for, or perform construction. Luis works as the homeowner’s independent advisor and representative.

Do you replace my architect or contractor?+

No. The architect leads design and the contractor leads construction. The owner’s representative helps the homeowner understand, coordinate, document, and evaluate the project from the owner’s side.

Do you inspect or certify the work?+

Site visits are observational and do not replace required inspections, testing, engineering, architectural services, or code enforcement. Visible concerns are documented for follow-up by the appropriate project professional.

Is contract review legal advice?+

No. Contract observations are provided from a construction-management perspective. Homeowners should use a qualified attorney for legal review and advice.

When should I bring you in?+

Ideally before selecting a contractor or signing a construction contract. Support can also begin during construction when a homeowner needs clarity, documentation, or independent guidance.

What projects are the best fit?+

Major renovations, additions, custom homes, ADUs, hillside projects, and other complex residential work where the decisions and financial exposure justify professional owner-side support.
